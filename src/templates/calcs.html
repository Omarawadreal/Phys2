Quick Review

Electric Field — Charged Sphere

Q — total charge on sphere  |  r — distance from centre to field point
E — electric field (points radially outward for +Q)
Formula: E = Q / (4πε₀r²)

Electric Field — Charged Disc

σ — surface charge density (C/m²)  |  R — disc radius
z — axial distance from disc centre to field point P
Formula: E = σ/(2ε₀) · [1 − z/√(z²+R²)]

Electric Field — Charged Ring

Q — total charge on ring  |  R — ring radius
z — axial distance from ring centre to field point P
By symmetry, only the axial component survives.
Formula: E = kQz / (z²+R²)^(3/2)

Electric Field — Infinite Plane

σ — surface charge density (C/m²)
Field is uniform and perpendicular to the plane on both sides.
Field magnitude is independent of distance.
Formula: E = σ / (2ε₀)

Coulomb's Law

q₁, q₂ — point charges  |  r — separation between them
F — electrostatic force (repulsive for like charges shown above)
Formula: F = k|q₁q₂| / r²   where k = 8.99 × 10⁹ N·m²/C²

Ohm's Law

V — voltage (Volts)  |  I — current (Amperes)  |  R — resistance (Ohms)
Formula: V = I · R — leave one field blank to solve for it.

Capacitance — Isolated Sphere

R — radius of the sphere
The reference (ground) conductor is at infinity.
Formula: C = 4πε₀R

Capacitance — Coaxial Cylinders

a — inner cylinder radius  |  b — outer cylinder radius
L — length of cylinders (b > a)
Formula: C = 2πε₀L / ln(b/a)

Capacitance — Parallel Plates

A — area of each plate  |  d — separation between plates
E — uniform field between the plates
Formula: C = ε₀A / d
